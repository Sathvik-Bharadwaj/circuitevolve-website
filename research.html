02 Why it matters

Beyond sizing, toward the whole analog flow.

NetGen extends the team's work from tuning an existing circuit to generating the circuit itself, with agents that treat a failed attempt as information rather than a dead end.

01

Orchestrated

Specialised agents coordinate on building an analog netlist.

02

Failure-aware

Failures are detected and fed back so later attempts route around them.
