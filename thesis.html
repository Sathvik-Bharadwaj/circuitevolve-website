Our thesis · the next era

Analog design. An open frontier.

Digital design became software. Analog design never made that transition, and it is still where silicon meets the real world.

01

The physical world is analog.

Analog circuits connect computation to signals, energy and the real world. Their behaviour depends on continuous, coupled choices: dimensions, bias points and compensation.

02

The search deserves better tools.

Engineers carry enormous knowledge into every design. Repetitive simulation and parameter tuning consume time that could be spent on architecture, constraints and new ideas.

An evolving loop-gain response. Illustrative simulation.
03

Computation can widen the frontier.

We see an opportunity to make circuit exploration a continuous, simulation-driven process. Automated search can evaluate alternatives while engineers guide the objectives and judge the evidence.

04

That is why circuitEvolve exists.

We are building toward an analog workflow where designers can explore more possibilities, understand the trade-offs, and move from specification toward silicon with greater confidence.
